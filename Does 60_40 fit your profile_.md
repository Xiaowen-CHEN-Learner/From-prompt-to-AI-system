**Shorter duration cuts interest-rate risk.** BSV, SHY, and VGSH-type funds hold bonds maturing within roughly 1-5 years, so their prices move far less when rates change. **Lower equity weight caps market-driven losses.** Trimming equity exposure from 60% to 25% substantially reduces the portfolio's sensitivity to a stock market correction, since VOO alone carries an annualized volatility near **15.24%** and a historical drawdown of **-24.8%**. A 5-year window can absorb a downturn better than 1-2 years, but a smaller equity sleeve still meaningfully lowers the odds of being underwater when the money is needed.

**Inflation protection without added rate risk.** VTIP's principal adjusts with CPI, so it defends purchasing power over 5 years without taking on the long-duration risk that hurt BND in 2022; its own volatility profile is modest at roughly **3.19%**.

**Minimal maintenance fits your passive style.** This is a static four-ETF, buy-and-hold structure — no active bond-picking, no sector rotation, and no need for daily attention.
